NEON YARD — COMBAT AUDIO

Recorded firearm sources: The Free Firearm Sound Library
Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney. CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Selected single shots trimmed at attack, downmixed to mono, low-pass filtered,
resampled to 32 kHz PCM, peak normalized and tail-faded for browser playback.
Exact source files and trim offsets: manifest.json beside this file.
Some in-game weapons use recordings of similar firearms, not the exact model.

Footsteps (snow, wood, concrete, grass): Kenney, Impact Sounds. CC0 1.0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Three variations per surface, supplied as original Ogg files.

Magazine and shell foley: Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero), Gun Reload Sound Effects.
clipload2.wav and singlebullet1.wav. CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sound-effects

Additional low-volume UI tones and fallback audio are synthesized by the game.
